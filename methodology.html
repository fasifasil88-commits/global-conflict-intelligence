Methodology

Updated 2026-10-29 · Created by Fasil Adattil

Publisher coverage index = 100 × distinct configured publishers with matching headlines in the last 24 hours ÷ 5. The configured publishers are Al Jazeera, BBC News, UN News, UN Geneva and Global Voices. BBC World and Middle East feeds count as one publisher. No alarm-word weights are used. This is a descriptive coverage measure, not a calibrated model of danger.

News is read from the existing KV-backed Worker; providers are checked by its 30-minute cron. Visitors refresh the cached snapshot, not every provider. News retention is seven days and the API is capped, so it is not comprehensive worldwide coverage. URL and exact normalized-headline duplicates are grouped while source links are retained.

A comparable news index is withheld for partial or stale coverage; a provider check older than 90 minutes is stale. Browser history uses a separate key for this method, with real observation times, and is not shared across devices. Earlier keyword-based scores are not mixed into it.

Shipping, oil and EASA metadata use a separate Pages endpoint with a six-hour read-through edge cache and background refresh on the next request. Cache is local to a Cloudflare data center and may be evicted. Observation dates, successful check times and delayed/cached states are shown. A real deployment snapshot is retained as a dated fallback.

Transit reduction = (baseline average − current average) / baseline average × 100. Current is a complete seven-calendar-day window; baseline is the latest complete non-overlapping 30-day window before it, with dates shown. Missing days are not filled with zero. AIS observations may undercount actual traffic; reduction alone does not establish a restriction or blockade. Oil is daily delayed spot data; EASA bulletins are not full NOTAM or flight-operation data.
