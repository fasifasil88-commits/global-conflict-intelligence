HOW TO INTERPRET SIGNALS

Severity is not certainty

Scores reflect the number of matching headlines and escalation-related terms. A high score may reflect heavy media coverage, not necessarily a worsening battlefield.

Verify major claims against multiple independent newsrooms or primary institutions. Headlines alone cannot confirm casualties, responsibility or military outcomes.
