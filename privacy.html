Privacy

Updated 2026-10-29 · Created by Fasil Adattil

This release adds no advertising, advertising identifiers or analytics scripts. The browser stores language/theme preferences, the last successful public news snapshot, comparison information and local coverage history. These records stay on that browser and can be removed with the dashboard’s clear-data control or browser settings.

Cloudflare hosts the site and processes normal connection information, such as IP address and request metadata, under its policies. When you load a map, requests also go to unpkg and OpenStreetMap; opening publisher or video links sends you to those external services. Their logs, cookies and policies are outside this project’s control.

Provider requests are made by server-side services; API credentials are not placed in the page. If analytics or ads are introduced later, the owner must update this policy and implement applicable disclosure/consent requirements before enabling them.
